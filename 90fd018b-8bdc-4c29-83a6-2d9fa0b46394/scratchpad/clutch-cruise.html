Tips from the bay

Garage Notes

Small things our team repeats to customers every week. Scroll across.

Timing

A night wash beats a noon wash

Soap dries fast on hot panels and leaves spots behind. After dark the paint is cool, the foam stays wet while it lifts dirt, and the rinse comes away clean.

Order

Interior first, exterior second

Beating out mats and vacuuming throws dust into the air. Do that before the body is wet and it won't settle straight back onto fresh paint.

Routine

How often is often enough

Through dusty months a fortnightly wash keeps grit from grinding into the clear coat. After rain, sooner — road film sets harder than dry dust.

Upkeep

Don't drive past the small knocks

A rattle or a weeping hose is cheap this month and expensive next. With a workshop in the same bay, it can be looked at while the car is already up.

Access

What 24 hours really buys you

No appointment and no queue. Come in after a long drive, or before an early start, and the car is ready when the day actually begins.
